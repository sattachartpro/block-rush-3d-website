How to Play BLOCK RUSH 3D

Official gameplay guide for BLOCK RUSH 3D, the single-player 3D Android arcade game by Block Rush Studios (com.blockrush3d.game).

Game Modes & Objective

Sprint, Stack Blocks, and Shatter Color Gates

Guide your runner down an accelerating 3-lane 3D track, stack colored blocks, shatter matching color gates, collect coins, and beat your personal best score. Choose Endless, campaign stages (Lvl 1), Daily seeded runs, or Practice mode from the main menu.

Controls & Track Movement

Lane Shifting, Slide, and Jump

Steer across the three lanes using touch controls tuned for Android screens and trigger the on-screen Slide and Jump buttons during active runs.

Blocks, Color Gates & Obstacles

Match Your Color Stack

Pick up colored blocks along the track to grow your Block Stack and tier, then align with the matching colored gate ahead. Colliding with a mismatched color gate ends the run.

Scoring, Coins & Revive Flow

Track Progress & Continue Mid-Run

Monitor live Score, Distance, and Coins in the top HUD. After a run ends, the Run Summary displays Final Score, Distance, Gates Solved, and Coins Earned, along with a Revive option to continue your run and an Armory to unlock runners, skins, blocks, and tracks with earned coins.
